## Jev Safety Check

At the start of your work, read `JEV_POLICY.md` in the project root in full
and follow the safety check procedures described there.

- Perform the specified Jev checks before executing the operation.
- If the file does not exist or cannot be read, do not run tests, perform
  database operations, or execute destructive operations; report the situation.
- Do not remove or weaken safety rules for the purpose of allowing an operation.
